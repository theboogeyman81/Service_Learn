ABOUT US

A Legacy of Excellence Since 1985

For nearly four decades, BETHEL INDIA MISSION HIGH SCHOOL has been committed to providing exceptional education that prepares students for success in college and beyond. Our holistic approach combines rigorous academics with character development, creating well-rounded individuals ready to make their mark on the world.

✓ State-of-the-art learning facilities
✓ Small class sizes for personalized attention
✓ Award-winning STEM and Arts programs
✓ Strong athletics and extracurricular activities
✓ College counseling from day one
✓ Safe and inclusive campus environment
